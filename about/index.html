About FileShrink

FileShrink is a free web service created to help students, job candidates, and professionals compress photos, signatures, and PDF documents to exact file sizes.

Who Runs FileShrink

FileShrink is independently maintained by a dedicated developer passionate about making web utilities fast, simple, and accessible on any mobile device or slow internet connection.

Why FileShrink Is Free

Applying for government jobs, exams, or university admissions should never require expensive software or paid subscriptions. FileShrink is 100% free with no signups, no usage caps, and no watermarks.

Our Privacy-First Promise

Every compression tool on FileShrink operates entirely inside your web browser. Your files never leave your phone or computer. No images, signatures, or documents are ever uploaded to any external server.
