Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add page numbers to pdf documents without losing selectable text?

FileShrink uses native PDF vector encoding to insert page numbers into your document so all original text and links remain 100% searchable and selectable.

Can I omit page numbering on the first cover page?

Yes. Simply check the 'Skip first page' option, and your cover page will stay unnumbered while the remaining pages are labeled neatly.

Is it safe to number confidential legal or academic PDFs here?

Yes, 100% safe. All numbering happens entirely in your local web browser. Your private documents are never sent over the internet to any server.
