How to Sign PDF Documents Electronically

In today's remote work environment, printing a contract, signing it with a pen, and scanning it back to computer is slow, wasteful, and outdated. Adding an electronic signature directly to your PDF lets you approve leases, vendor agreements, NDAs, timesheets, and application forms in seconds.

An electronic signature image stamp allows you to draw your signature using your finger on a smartphone touchscreen or mouse on a computer, type your name using elegant cursive handwriting scripts, or upload a pre-scanned signature file. You can then drag and resize the signature stamp anywhere on the document.

Because contracts and financial agreements contain sensitive personal and corporate data, FileShrink processes your signature entirely inside your browser. Your signature and document are never uploaded to our servers or stored in any database.

FAQ

Is an electronic signature legally valid for business contracts?

In most jurisdictions (including under the US ESIGN Act and EU eIDAS regulations), electronic signatures are recognized as legally binding for commercial agreements, leases, and routine business contracts.

How can I make my drawn signature look smooth on a computer?

For the smoothest results, open this page on your smartphone or tablet and draw your signature using your finger or stylus, or use our elegant cursive font typing mode.

Can I apply my signature to multiple pages at once?

Yes. You can choose to place the signature on the current page, the last page, all pages, or a specific range of pages.

Will adding a signature increase my PDF file size significantly?

No. The signature stamp is embedded as an optimized vector or transparent image layer that adds only a negligible few kilobytes to the document.

Can I sign password-protected PDF files?

You will need to enter the document password to unlock the file before adding your signature so the browser can render and save the signed PDF.
