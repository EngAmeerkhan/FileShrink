Protecting PDF Documents with Custom Watermarks

Watermarking is the standard way to protect sensitive drafts, proprietary business proposals, confidential medical records, and copyrighted manuscripts from unauthorized distribution or plagiarism. Prominent watermarks like 'CONFIDENTIAL', 'DRAFT', 'SAMPLE', or your company name immediately establish document status.

Effective watermarks require precise opacity and positioning. If a watermark is too dark, it obscures underlying text and makes the document difficult to read; if it is too faint, unauthorized recipients can easily ignore it. A semi-transparent diagonal watermark across the center of each page offers the best balance of visibility and text readability.

FileShrink lets you customize watermark text, font size, rotation angle, and opacity level with real-time visual previews. Everything runs client-side, ensuring your confidential trade secrets and draft agreements never touch external servers.

FAQ

What opacity level works best for PDF watermarks?

An opacity between 15% and 25% is recommended. This makes the watermark clearly noticeable while ensuring that the underlying body text remains completely readable.

Can someone easily remove this watermark from the PDF?

Our tool permanently embeds the watermark layer into the PDF document streams, preventing casual readers from simply clicking and deleting the text.

Can I watermark only specific pages or the entire document?

You can apply the watermark across all pages in the PDF or target specific page ranges depending on your project requirements.

Will watermarking increase my PDF file size?

Text-based watermarks add only a few kilobytes of stream data, keeping your file lightweight and easy to share via email.

Are my confidential documents private while watermarking?

Yes, 100%. The watermarking engine runs locally in your browser memory. Your documents are never sent across the internet.
