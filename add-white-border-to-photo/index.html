Frequently Asked Questions

How can I add white border to photo files online?

It takes only seconds to add white border to photo files and make your feed look professional. Simply upload your photo, drag the border thickness slider, and download the finished result immediately.

Can I add border to photo without changing the total image size?

Yes. Turning on "Keep the original total size" scales your photo inward within the border, so the final exported image dimensions remain identical to your original upload.

Can I choose colors other than white for my photo border?

Yes, you can pick black, minimalist gray, or any custom color palette to match your aesthetic theme.
