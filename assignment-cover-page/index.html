Frequently Asked Questions

How do I merge this cover page in front of my existing assignment PDF?

Download your cover page as a PDF, then open our free Merge PDF Files tool. Place your cover page as the first file and your assignment body as the second file to combine them into one document.

Does this cover page maker support non-English text like Urdu or Arabic?

Yes. Our high-resolution canvas engine automatically detects right-to-left scripts such as Urdu and Arabic, preserving proper character shaping and bidirectional alignment.

Can I print this cover page directly on standard A4 printer paper?

Yes. The canvas renders at 300 DPI A4 print standard (2480 x 3508 pixels). You can click Print to send it straight to your desktop printer or download the crisp vector-fitted PDF.
