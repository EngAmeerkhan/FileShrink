Frequently Asked Questions

How many consecutive lectures do I need to attend after taking sick leave?

The tool uses the target recovery formula: Consecutive Classes Needed = ⌈(Required % × Held - Attended) ÷ (1 - Required %)⌉. It calculates the exact number of back-to-back classes you must attend without absence.

Can attendance be calculated if my course includes laboratory sessions?

Yes. If labs carry equal attendance weight, sum lab sessions into classes held and attended. If labs are calculated separately by your department, run the calculator once for lectures and once for lab hours.

What happens if I miss a class after reaching the minimum attendance percentage?

Enter the total planned classes for your course into the optional box. The calculator will determine exactly how many more classes you can safely miss while keeping your attendance above your institution threshold.
