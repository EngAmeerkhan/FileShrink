Frequently Asked Questions

How does this black and white photo converter calculate grayscale values?

Use this browser-based black and white photo converter to turn any picture into a timeless monochrome portrait. It applies the ITU-R BT.709 luminance standard (0.2126 R + 0.7152 G + 0.0722 B) to faithfully capture human visual tonal depth.

Can I create a grayscale image online and keep transparent backgrounds?

Yes. If you upload a transparent PNG logo or graphic, you can convert it to a grayscale image online while maintaining transparent cutouts by selecting PNG output.

What is the high contrast threshold mode used for?

Threshold turns mid-tone grays into pure 100% black or pure white. This is ideal for extracting black signatures from paper scans or making high-contrast artistic prints.
