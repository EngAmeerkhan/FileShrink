FAQ

How do I blur image online without uploading my photo to a server?

You can blur image online right in your browser to conceal confidential text, car number plates, or personal faces. All pixel math runs in client-side JavaScript, ensuring your files never leave your device.

How can I blur face in photo or license plates?

Select the "Blur an area" option, then click and drag rectangles or circles directly across sensitive faces or ID numbers. Only the selected areas will be blurred, leaving the rest of your photo unchanged.

Does blurring the whole image remove metadata and background details?

Yes. When downloading the output, original camera EXIF tags (such as GPS location) are stripped automatically, providing full privacy protection.
