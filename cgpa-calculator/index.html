Frequently Asked Questions

How is CGPA different from GPA?

GPA measures your academic performance in a single semester or term. CGPA (Cumulative Grade Point Average) combines all your completed semesters together, weighted by total credit hours.

What should I do if my college gives only GPAs without credit hours?

Turn on the toggle 'My university does not use credit hours'. The calculator will then compute an unweighted average of your semester GPAs directly.

How is the required next semester GPA calculated?

The tool uses the target cumulative average formula: Needed GPA = [Target × (Current Credits + Next Credits) - Current Grade Points] ÷ Next Credits. If the needed score exceeds your scale maximum, it lets you know immediately.
