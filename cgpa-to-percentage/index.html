Frequently Asked Questions

Why is there no single standard formula for converting CGPA to percentage?

Academic evaluation systems differ globally. Some institutions use direct proportional scaling (CGPA / Max × 100), while others assign custom multipliers based on historical grading distributions.

What is an institution multiplier, and where do I find mine?

A multiplier is a fixed number specified by your college or university on the back of your official transcript. If your transcript states a conversion factor, enter that value in the custom multiplier box.

How do I convert percentage back to CGPA for international applications?

Toggle the 'Convert Percentage to CGPA' switch. The tool will calculate your equivalent CGPA on your chosen scale (such as a 4.0 or 10.0 scale) using the reverse conversion formula.
