How to Put CNIC Front and Back on One Page

  1. Upload your ID card front photo in the first box and the back photo in the second box.
  2. Adjust rotation, zoom, card size, and optionally type your purpose note.
  3. Click "Download as PDF" for a ready-to-print A4 sheet or "Download as JPG".

Frequently Asked Questions

How does this tool help make a cnic copy print on one page?

Instead of manually aligning photos in Word, FileShrink neatly centers both front and back cards on a clean A4 sheet ready for your printer.

Can I use this for driving licenses and student cards too?

Yes. Any standard two-sided card (such as a driving license, office badge, or student ID) can be combined on a single sheet using this layout tool.

Is my ID card data kept private when using this website?

Yes, 100% private. All rendering and PDF compilation occur directly within your web browser. Your card pictures are never transmitted to any external server.
