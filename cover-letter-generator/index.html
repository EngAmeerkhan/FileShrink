Frequently Asked Questions

Why should I use an online cover letter generator instead of writing from scratch?

A cover letter generator organizes your contact information, recipient details, and body paragraphs into a standard format. It saves you time formatting so you can focus on tailoring your skills.

How long should a cover letter be?

Keep your cover letter between 250 and 400 words, fitting cleanly on a single page. Employers review many applications and appreciate concise letters that focus directly on relevant skills.

Should I submit my cover letter as a PDF or text file?

A PDF document is recommended because it locks your formatting, margins, and typography on every computer or phone. Only use plain text if an online application form specifically asks for it.
