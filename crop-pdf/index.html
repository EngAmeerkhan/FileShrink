Frequently Asked Questions

How do I crop pdf pages without losing text sharpness?

By default, FileShrink updates the PDF MediaBox and CropBox coordinates directly. All fonts, vector text, and graphics remain native and sharp with zero rasterization.

What does "Cropped-out content is hidden, not deleted" mean?

Standard PDF cropping hides content outside the crop border during viewing and printing. If you need sensitive data permanently purged, enable "Remove the cropped area permanently" to rasterize the page.

Can I apply different margins to different page sizes?

Yes. The specified margin values (e.g. 50 pt on all sides) are subtracted from each page's individual dimensions, so mixed page sizes in the same file are trimmed evenly.
