How to Extract Specific Pages from Large PDF Files

Large PDF files frequently contain only a few pages that you actually need. You might want to extract a single chapter from an academic textbook, save a signed signature page from a 50-page mortgage packet, or isolate monthly invoices from an annual financial statement.

Extracting pages creates a brand-new, lightweight PDF containing only the pages you specify. This makes it effortless to email specific sections to clients or colleagues without sending massive, confidential, or irrelevant document sections.

FileShrink gives you visual thumbnail previews of all pages. Simply click the pages you want to extract or enter custom page numbers (like 1, 4, 7-12) to generate a clean, standalone PDF in seconds directly on your device.

FAQ

Can I extract non-consecutive pages (such as pages 2, 5, and 9)?

Yes. You can select any combination of non-consecutive pages or page ranges, and the tool will combine them into a single clean PDF.

Will extracted pages lose text sharpness or formatting?

No. Extracted pages retain 100% of their original vector quality, embedded images, font styles, and hyperlinks.

Does extracting pages alter my original PDF file?

No. Your original file remains untouched on your device. The tool generates a new, separate PDF containing only your selected pages.

Can I extract pages on my mobile phone?

Yes. The visual thumbnail selector works smoothly on touchscreen smartphones, iPads, and desktop computers alike.

Is there any limit on how many pages I can extract?

No. You can extract pages from documents of any length, completely free of charge and without signups.
