Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate my GPA if my university uses a different grading scale?

Open the Customize Grade Scale section below the calculator. Edit the grade point values to match your university's exact syllabus. The calculator updates immediately with your custom values.

What should I do with repeat courses or pass/fail subjects?

For repeat courses, enter only the final recognized grade and credit hours according to your department rules. If a course is ungraded pass/fail with 0 quality points, omit it or do not include its credits in weighted GPA.

Can I use this GPA calculator for scholarship and job applications?

Yes. This tool computes standard semester grade point averages. You can copy the exact GPA, total earned credits, and grade points directly for verification in application forms.
