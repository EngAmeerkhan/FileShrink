The 12 Islamic Months

Each lunar month in the Hijri calendar spans either 29 or 30 days based on the lunar cycle:

  1. Muharram (Sacred Month)
  2. Safar
  3. Rabi al-Awwal
  4. Rabi al-Thani
  5. Jumada al-Awwal
  6. Jumada al-Thani
  7. Rajab (Sacred Month)
  8. Shaban
  9. Ramadan (Month of Fasting)
  10. Shawwal (Eid al-Fitr)
  11. Dhu al-Qadah (Sacred Month)
  12. Dhu al-Hijjah (Month of Hajj & Eid al-Adha)

Local Moon Sighting Adjustment

Depending on weather conditions, cloud cover, and regional latitude, religious authorities in countries like Pakistan, India, the United Kingdom, or Morocco may declare the start of a month one or two days earlier or later. Understanding the Islamic Lunar Calendar and Date Conversion

The Islamic Hijri calendar is a purely lunar calendar consisting of 12 lunar months in a year of 354 or 355 days. It began in the year 622 CE when Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) migrated from Mecca to Medina, an event known as the Hijrah.

Because a lunar year is approximately 11 days shorter than the solar Gregorian year (365 days), Islamic calendar dates gradually cycle through all four seasons over a 33-year period. This is why holy observances—such as Ramadan, Eid al-Fitr, Eid al-Adha, and the Hajj pilgrimage—occur roughly 10 to 12 days earlier each solar year.

FAQ

Why can Islamic Hijri dates differ by one day between countries?

Islamic months begin with the physical sighting of the crescent moon (hilal). Due to weather, cloud cover, and geographical coordinates, religious authorities in different countries may declare the month start on different days.

What is the Umm al-Qura calendar?

The Umm al-Qura calendar is the official astronomical lunar calendar calculated and published by Saudi Arabia, used widely across the Middle East as an administrative calendar standard.

What are the four sacred months in the Islamic calendar?

The four sacred months (Al-Ashhur Al-Hurum) mentioned in the Quran are Muharram, Rajab, Dhu al-Qadah, and Dhu al-Hijjah.

Can I convert dates backwards from Hijri to Gregorian?

Yes. Our converter works both ways: Gregorian to Hijri and Hijri to Gregorian with accurate weekday matching.

Can I convert historical dates from centuries ago?

Yes. The astronomical mathematical algorithm converts historical dates throughout Islamic history accurately.
