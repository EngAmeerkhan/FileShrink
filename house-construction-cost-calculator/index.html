Frequently Asked Questions

How does this house construction cost calculator estimate total building expenses?

The tool converts your plot measurements into total covered square footage across all floors, then multiplies that area by your local contractor rates for grey structure and finishing before adding optional project fees and contingency buffers.

What is the difference between grey structure and finishing costs?

Grey structure covers foundational concrete, brick masonry, plaster, and basic pipework. Finishing includes flooring tiles, paint, woodwork, kitchen cabinetry, sanitary fixtures, and electrical fittings.

Why should I add an extra contingency buffer percentage to my estimate?

Material prices like steel and cement fluctuate during construction, and unexpected on-site adjustments often occur. Adding a 5% to 15% buffer ensures your budget remains realistic from ground-breaking to handover.
