Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best instagram profile picture size in pixels?

Format your avatar to the exact instagram profile picture size with a live circular preview. Uploading at 640 x 640 px ensures that your picture remains crisp on Retina mobile screens and web profiles.

How does the circular preview overlay help position my avatar?

Instagram displays all profile pictures in a round circle. The circular guide helps you keep your face right in the center so hair or chin details are never cut off unexpectedly.

Which Instagram post sizes are supported?

You can easily switch to standard feed post presets: Square 1:1 (1080 x 1080 px), Portrait 4:5 (1080 x 1350 px), and Landscape 1.91:1 (1080 x 566 px).
