Frequently Asked Questions

How does this invoice generator track partial payments and balance due?

Entering a figure into the Amount Paid input automatically recalculates the remaining Balance Due on the invoice generator and updates the summary table on the invoice sheet.

How are due dates calculated for net 15 or net 30 invoices?

The system adds the selected number of days to the invoice creation date, correctly factoring in differing month lengths and leap years so payment deadlines are always accurate.

Can I download my invoice as an image or print directly?

Yes. Besides vector PDF export, you can download a full-resolution JPG image or print directly using the clean print styling that hides all tool controls.
