Frequently Asked Questions

How does this loan calculator differ from a standard emi calculator?

This loan calculator calculates equal periodic repayments using the standard amortization formula just like an emi calculator, while additionally computing early payoff savings from extra monthly prepayments.

Can I use this as a monthly payment calculator for auto and personal loans?

Yes. This tool operates as a versatile monthly payment calculator suitable for personal loans, auto loans, mortgages, and consumer financing with terms up to 600 months.

How does adding an extra monthly payment reduce my total interest and loan term?

Every extra monthly payment directly diminishes your outstanding principal balance, reducing future compound interest and shortening your total repayment duration.
