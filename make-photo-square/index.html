FAQ

How do I make photo square without cropping?

You can make photo square in one click without cutting off heads or side details. By choosing "Keep the whole photo", the tool expands the shorter dimension and fills the borders with a blurred or solid color background.

When should I choose a square crop image instead of adding padding?

If your subject is right in the center and you want a clean borderless 1:1 graphic, selecting square crop lets you frame the focal area perfectly.

Does adding background padding reduce photo quality?

No, your photo is placed at full native resolution onto the square canvas, preserving maximum image clarity.
