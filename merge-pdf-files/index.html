How to Merge PDF Files

  1. Upload two or more PDF documents by clicking "Choose PDF Files".
  2. Reorder documents using the arrow buttons or drag them into position.
  3. Click "Merge Files Now" and download your single merged PDF.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I combine pdf files into a single organized document?

Upload all your PDF documents at once, use the up and down arrow buttons or drag them to set the desired order, then click Merge PDF.

Can I merge PDF into one file without losing text selection?

Yes, our client-side engine uses pdf-lib to merge original PDF streams directly, keeping all vector text selectable and searchable.

What if the combined merged PDF is too large for my upload portal?

After merging, you can easily click our 'Make it smaller' shortcut to compress your combined PDF to 500KB, 1MB, or 2MB.
