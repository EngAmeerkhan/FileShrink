Official Guidelines for Passport Photo Dimensions & Pixel Sizes

Getting your passport photo rejected can cause weeks of stressful travel and visa delays. Passport agencies and visa consulates have strict geometric rules: US passports and visas require a 2 by 2 inch photo (600 by 600 pixels at 300 DPI), while UK, Schengen, and Commonwealth countries mandate a 35 by 45 mm photo (approximately 413 by 531 pixels).

Beyond raw pixel dimensions, your head must occupy between 50% and 69% of the total image height from the bottom of the chin to the top of the head. Your eyes should be open and positioned roughly two-thirds of the way up the photo. You must look directly into the camera with a neutral expression and mouth closed.

FAQ

What are the exact pixel dimensions for a 2x2 inch US passport photo?

A standard 2x2 inch passport photo requires 600 by 600 pixels at 300 DPI, with a square 1:1 aspect ratio.

What pixel dimensions are required for UK, Schengen, and Indian passport photos?

These regions require a 35 by 45 mm photo, which translates to 413 by 531 pixels at standard 300 DPI print resolution.

What background color is mandatory for official passport photos?

Most countries require a plain, smooth white or off-white background with no patterns, shadows, or objects behind your head.

Can I wear eyeglasses or headwear in my passport photo?

Eyeglasses are no longer permitted in US passport photos and most visa applications. Religious headwear is permitted provided your full face from chin to forehead is clearly visible.

Can I print my resized passport photo at home or a local pharmacy?

Yes. You can save the high-resolution file and print it on glossy photo paper at home or at any commercial photo print kiosk.
