Frequently Asked Questions

What is the standard passport photo size in pixels at 300 DPI?

For photo size 35x45mm at 300 DPI, the pixel resolution is 413x531 px. For 2x2 inches, it is 600x600 px. Please use the size your form asks for.

Can I use this as a cnic size photo maker and passport photo size in cm?

Yes, you can choose custom dimensions in cm, mm, or inches and adjust DPI to match your specific government identity card portal.

Does this tool remove the background from my picture?

This tool crops and resizes. It does not remove backgrounds. Please take your photo against a plain white wall.
