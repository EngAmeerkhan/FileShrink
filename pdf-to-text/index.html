Extracting Plain Text from PDF Documents

PDFs are designed for consistent visual presentation, but getting raw text out of them for editing, research, or data entry can be frustrating. Copying text manually often introduces weird line breaks, missing spaces, and garbled symbols.

Our PDF to Text tool extracts clean, unformatted plain text directly from native PDF text layers. You can quickly copy essay paragraphs into Microsoft Word, import tabular data into spreadsheets, or feed research papers into text analysis and AI tools.

Because extraction runs locally in your browser, you can extract text from sensitive business proposals, client records, and legal briefs with complete peace of mind, knowing your confidential text is never logged or stored on any server.

FAQ

Can this tool extract text from scanned paper PDFs?

This tool extracts digital text streams from native PDFs. If your PDF is a flat image scan from a physical printer, you will need an OCR (optical character recognition) tool to recognize text from pixels.

Why does extracted text sometimes have unusual line breaks?

PDFs position words using exact geometric coordinates on a page rather than continuous paragraphs. Our tool intelligently reconstructs sentences to provide clean, readable text.

Can I copy all extracted text with a single click?

Yes. Click the 'Copy Text' button to immediately copy the entire extracted document to your clipboard, or download it as a clean .txt file.

Does this tool work on multi-column research papers?

Yes. The extraction algorithm parses text streams sequentially to maintain readable reading order across multi-column layouts.

Is there a limit on document length?

You can extract text from documents spanning dozens or hundreds of pages without restrictions or fees.
