Frequently Asked Questions

How is percentage increase or decrease calculated?

Percentage change subtracts the initial number from the final number, divides the difference by the absolute initial value, and multiplies by 100. A positive outcome indicates an increase, while negative indicates a decrease.

How do I calculate exam marks percentage from practical and theory scores?

Use the 'Marks Percentage' tab. Add up your obtained scores in theory and practicals for the first box, then sum the maximum available marks for the second box. The tool divides obtained by total.

Can this tool handle decimal values and fractions?

Yes. You can enter any positive or negative decimal numbers. Calculations preserve full mathematical precision and round only for display without scientific notation.
