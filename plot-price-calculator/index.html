FAQ

How does this plot price calculator handle differing land measurement units?

The calculator harmonizes measurements by converting your selected area and rate basis into square feet, accurately accounting for regional marla standards before calculating final totals.

Why is it important to verify the local marla size when calculating plot prices?

In Pakistan, standard revenue land measurement uses 272.25 square feet per marla, whereas schemes like LDA in Lahore often adopt 225 square feet, and Islamabad CDA societies use 250 square feet.

How do development charges and discounts affect the total plot cost?

Development charges add external infrastructure costs to the base land value, while discount percentages subtract promotional deductions before presenting the final payable price.
