Frequently Asked Questions

Why is timestamp-based timing more accurate than counting seconds?

Browsers often throttle JavaScript timers in background tabs to save power. Our tool calculates remaining time by comparing the exact current system timestamp against the target end time, ensuring 100% precision.

How many Pomodoro sessions should I do before taking a long break?

The classic technique recommends four consecutive 25-minute focus intervals separated by short 5-minute pauses, followed by an extended 15 to 30-minute restorative break.

Does the timer keep running if I switch tabs or lock my phone screen?

Yes. The timer updates live in your browser tab title. You can also turn on the 'Keep screen on' toggle on supported devices so your display stays awake during your study session.
