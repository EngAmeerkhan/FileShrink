Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 8, 2026

At FileShrink (accessible from https://fileshrink.vercel.app), visitor privacy is our highest priority. This Privacy Policy details our local data processing principles, cookie policies, and advertising disclosures.

1. Zero-Upload Local Processing Policy

FileShrink uses client-side web technologies (HTML5 Canvas and JavaScript). All compression and resizing of images, signatures, and PDF files happens directly inside your web browser. Your files are never transmitted to our servers, never stored in databases, and never viewed by anyone.

2. Cookies and Local Storage

FileShrink uses standard browser cookies and localStorage solely to remember your UI preferences (such as dismissing the cookie banner) and enhance site performance.

3. Google AdSense and Third-Party Advertising

Google is a third-party vendor on FileShrink. It uses cookies, such as the DART cookie, to serve advertisements to visitors based upon their visits to this and other websites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or through the Digital Advertising Alliance Consumer Choice page.

4. Analytics and Server Logs

Like most websites, our web host may collect basic anonymous log data (including browser type, operating system, referring URL, and timestamp). This information is used solely for technical diagnostics and contains no personal identity data.

5. Children's Privacy

FileShrink does not collect personal information from any user, including children under 13 years of age.
