FAQ

How does the tool identify duplicate lines in my text?

The algorithm parses your text line by line, comparing character strings. You can choose whether letter casing (e.g. 'Apple' vs 'apple') is treated as identical or unique.

Can I deduplicate my list while preserving the original line order?

Yes. The tool keeps the first occurrence of each unique line in its original position while stripping all subsequent duplicates.

Does the tool remove invisible spaces and empty blank lines?

Yes. You can toggle options to trim accidental whitespace around words and automatically eliminate empty blank lines.

How large of a text file or list can I paste into this tool?

Our optimized browser algorithm processes lists containing tens of thousands of lines smoothly in a fraction of a second.

Is my customer data or email list uploaded to any server?

No. The deduplication runs entirely inside your browser memory using local JavaScript. Your data remains 100% private.
