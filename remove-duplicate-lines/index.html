Frequently Asked Questions

How does this tool remove duplicate lines from lists?

The tool uses an in-memory hash set to evaluate each line sequentially. When you remove duplicate lines with our utility, repeated elements are filtered out instantly according to your selected sensitivity options.

What is the difference between keeping the first or last occurrence?

Selecting “Keep first occurrence” keeps the first time a line appeared and discards subsequent repeats. Selecting “Keep last occurrence” tracks newer entries down the file and retains their later position.

Will this tool freeze on massive lists of 50,000 or 100,000 lines?

No. The deduplication algorithm operates in single-pass linear time O(N), allowing typical 100,000-line datasets to process in under 100 milliseconds inside your browser.
