Frequently Asked Questions

How do I use the signature resizer for online government and exam forms?

Upload a photo of your handwritten signature, pick standard dimensions like 140x60 px or 200x80 px, select a target size such as 20 KB, and download your resized signature image.

Why does my transparent PNG signature turn black and how does FileShrink fix it?

Standard JPEG conversions can turn transparent backgrounds black. Our signature resizer includes an automatic white background option to keep your signature clean and readable.

Is my digital signature kept private and safe on FileShrink?

Yes, absolutely. The signature resizer runs 100% in your browser. Your signature image is never sent to any server or stored online.
