How to Split PDF Pages

  1. Upload your PDF document by tapping "Choose PDF".
  2. Select the pages you want by tapping thumbnails or typing page numbers.
  3. Click "Split PDF" to save your new PDF document or ZIP package.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the split pdf tool keep text and fonts selectable?

Yes, our vector PDF engine preserves all native fonts and vector content so text remains 100% searchable and selectable.

Can I split custom page intervals like 1-3, 5, 8-10?

Yes, you can click thumbnails directly or type any combination of page numbers and ranges into the range box.

How do I split every page into its own separate PDF?

Select 'Every page as a separate PDF' and click Split PDF to download all individual page files packed in a ZIP archive.
