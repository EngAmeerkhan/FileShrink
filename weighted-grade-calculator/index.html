Frequently Asked Questions

How does this tool function as a weighted average calculator for academic courses?

By multiplying each assignment score by its assigned syllabus weight and summing the points, this tool functions as an exact weighted average calculator for university and high school classes.

What happens if my category weights do not add up to 100 percent?

The calculator displays both your cumulative points earned out of the entered weight and normalizes the final score to an equivalent 100 percent scale.

Can I enter points earned out of total points possible instead of percentages?

Yes. Switch the grade toggle from percentage to 'Points Earned / Possible' to input raw homework or quiz points without having to pre-calculate percentages.
