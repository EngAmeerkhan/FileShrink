FAQ

How does a WhatsApp click-to-chat link work?

It uses the official `https://wa.me/` protocol. When clicked, it instructs WhatsApp to open a direct chat window with that specific phone number without requiring the user to save it in their address book.

Why should I omit the plus sign and leading zero from the phone number?

The `wa.me` standard requires pure numeric digits in international format (e.g. `923001234567` instead of `+92-0300-1234567`). Our tool automatically strips plus signs, spaces, and leading zeros for you.

Can I include emojis and punctuation in the pre-filled greeting message?

Yes. You can include standard text, punctuation, and emojis. The tool URL-encodes special characters so your message appears perfectly formatted in the customer's chat bar.

Where are the best places to share my WhatsApp link and QR code?

Add it to your Instagram bio link, TikTok profile, website header, business card, product packaging, restaurant table cards, and email signatures for instant customer engagement.

Do these generated WhatsApp links or QR codes ever expire?

Never. Because they link directly to your personal or business phone number without using intermediary redirect servers, they remain permanently active.
