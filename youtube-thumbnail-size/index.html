Mastering YouTube Thumbnail Dimensions & Click-Through Optimization

Your thumbnail is the single most important factor determining whether a viewer clicks your video or scrolls past it in YouTube search results and home feeds. According to Google's official developer guidelines, the ideal YouTube thumbnail size is 1280 pixels wide by 720 pixels high, with a 16:9 widescreen aspect ratio, a minimum width of 640 pixels, and a file size under 2 MB.

When designing thumbnails, always account for mobile viewing. More than 70% of YouTube watch time happens on smartphones, where thumbnails appear relatively small. Use bold, high-contrast typography, close-up facial expressions, and vibrant background colors that stand out in dark mode.

Keep the lower-right corner of your thumbnail clear of essential text or faces. YouTube places the black video duration timestamp box (such as '12:45') in the bottom-right corner, which will obscure any text or details placed in that corner. FAQ

What are the exact dimensions and aspect ratio for YouTube thumbnails?

The official standard is 1280 by 720 pixels with a 16:9 aspect ratio, saved in JPG, PNG, or WEBP format.

What is the maximum file size allowed for YouTube thumbnails?

YouTube enforces a strict 2 MB file size limit for custom video thumbnails. Images larger than 2 MB will be rejected by YouTube Studio.

Why shouldn't I place text in the bottom-right corner of my thumbnail?

YouTube displays the video length timestamp badge over the bottom-right corner. Any text, logo, or facial detail placed there will be hidden beneath the badge.

How can I make my thumbnail text readable on mobile phones?

Use heavy, bold sans-serif fonts with high contrast (such as bright yellow or white text with dark drop shadows or outlines) and limit overlay text to 3 to 5 key words.
